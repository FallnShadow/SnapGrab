SnapGrab Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to SnapGrab. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our website.

2. Information We Collect

SnapGrab may process video URLs submitted by visitors to provide supported services. We do not require users to create an account.

3. URL Processing

Submitted URLs are sent to our server for validation and processing. Server logs or third-party services may process technical information such as IP addresses and request details.

4. Cookies

SnapGrab does not intentionally use advertising cookies at this stage. Hosting or third-party services may use their own technologies.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may use Cloudflare and other service providers. Their own privacy policies may apply.

6. Data Retention

We do not intentionally maintain a personal library of submitted URLs or downloaded files. However, temporary processing data and server logs may be retained by service providers.

7. Security

We use HTTPS and reasonable technical measures to protect information processed through our website.

8. User Rights

Users may contact us with questions or requests concerning their personal information.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the SnapGrab website administrator.
